Data and reproducibility

Representations and provenance

Documented formats connect image processing with downstream models while retaining the information needed to inspect an experiment.

Read the workflow guide →
01

Indexed patch archives

Pixels, magnification, level-zero coordinates and valid bounds are stored in an HDF5 archive per slide.

02

Explicit model inputs

Feature bags, spatial graphs and aligned hierarchies have distinct contracts. Model access and runtime requirements are documented by profile.

03

Ordered feature storage

AnnData stores feature vectors with patch metadata. Compatible reruns reuse completed vectors and retain their source identities.
